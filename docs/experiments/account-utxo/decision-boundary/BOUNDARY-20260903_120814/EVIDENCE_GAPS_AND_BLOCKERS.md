# Evidence Gaps and Blockers

## Documentary integrity

The old execution-package inventory/provenance defect is repaired. Its current
receipt binds the exact governed inventory and records a documentary pass.
That repair supplies no candidate evidence and does not rehabilitate the stale
historical structural result or its unbound transcripts.

| Cross-gate | substantive Account/UTXO symmetry | mirrored slots cannot establish semantic equivalence |

Templates, schemas, matrices, structural arithmetic, label counts, lint
passes, source searches, code-health checks, and documentary receipts do not
substitute for candidate behavior or quantitative evidence.

Protected inputs belong to the owner or applicable protocol authority.
Implementation and evidence collection require separately authorized,
candidate-symmetric work. Account and UTXO remain co-equal and unranked.
